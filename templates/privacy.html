Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 20, 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create an account and use Expenzo, we collect the information you provide directly, such as your email address and password. We also collect the expense data you enter, including amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions, so that the Service can display your transaction history and spending summaries back to you.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the information you provide to operate and improve Expenzo, including:

  • Authenticating you and keeping your account secure
  • Displaying your expenses, categories, and spending summaries
  • Communicating with you about your account, such as password resets
  • Diagnosing technical issues and improving the Service

We do not sell your personal data or expense information to third parties.

3. Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored in the Service's database and are only accessible to you when you are signed in. We take reasonable measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, alteration, or loss, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure. You are responsible for keeping your login credentials confidential.

4. Third Party Services

Expenzo may rely on third-party infrastructure providers (such as hosting and font services) to operate the Service. These providers may process limited technical data, such as IP addresses, as part of delivering their services, but they do not receive access to your expense data beyond what is necessary to run the application. We do not share your personal data with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us through the contact details provided within the app. We are happy to address any concerns about your data.
